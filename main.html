Modern Architecture

Modern architecture emphasizes simplicity, functionality, and clean lines. It often uses materials such as glass, steel, and concrete to create beautiful and practical spaces. Natural light and open floor plans are important features of modern architectural design.

Sustainable Architecture

Sustainable architecture focuses on reducing environmental impact. Architects use energy-efficient designs, natural ventilation, renewable materials, and green spaces to make buildings more environmentally friendly. These methods help conserve resources and improve the quality of life.
